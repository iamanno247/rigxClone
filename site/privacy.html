Privacy

What happens to the details you send us through the forms on this site. Short version: we use them only to reply to you, we don't sell them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

What we collect

  • Brand inquiries: your name, company, email, phone (optional), budget, timeline, goals and message.
  • Creator applications: your name, creator tag, email, phone (optional), platform, follower range, channel link, games, language and city.
  • To stop spam and abuse: a scrambled (one-way hashed) version of your IP address and your browser type. We never store your IP address itself.

The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Signed-in staff of RIGX use one security cookie for the admin area only.

Why

Only to reply to your inquiry or application and, if we work together, to run the campaign. We collect these details with your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.

Who else handles it

  • Our hosting and database providers, which store the site and the form entries on our behalf.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a form is sent by a person, not a bot.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the site's typefaces.

We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't share them with brands or creators without asking you first.

How long we keep it

Form entries are kept while we're in touch with you and for up to 24 months after, then deleted. Spam-protection records are deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, at any time. Email hello@rigx.in and we'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Keeping it safe

Everything is sent over HTTPS. Only signed-in RIGX staff, using 2-step verification, can see form entries, and every change they make is logged.
